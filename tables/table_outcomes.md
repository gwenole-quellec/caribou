# CARIBOU restriction outcomes at 50% pooling

Median percentages across 10,000 sampled prioritization rules. Each cell shows the median outcome at the selected pooling fraction followed by the median **within-rule paired difference** relative to no pooling (lambda = 0), in percentage points. Positive differences indicate more restrictions.

Severe means refusing at least 50% of an active traveler-year's requested missions; repeated means refusals in at least two distinct years among travelers active in at least two years.
